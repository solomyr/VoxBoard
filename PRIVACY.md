# VoxBoard Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

VoxBoard is a keyboard and dictation app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It is
built so that your voice and your text stay on your devices.

## What VoxBoard does not collect

VoxBoard does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data.
There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no
third-party SDKs that collect data.

## Your voice and text

- Speech recognition and AI text cleanup run entirely on your device, using
  models stored on your device. Your recordings and text are never uploaded.
- With Apple Intelligence turned on, VoxBoard may ask Apple's on-device
  language model to choose between swipe-typing suggestions. This runs on your
  device under Apple's privacy terms; VoxBoard sends nothing to its own servers.
- Recordings and transcripts you make are kept on your device in VoxBoard's
  History so you can review them. You choose how long they are kept (24 hours by
  default) and can delete them at any time in Settings.
- The keyboard learns words you type or swipe to improve suggestions. This
  stays in the keyboard's storage on your device and is never transmitted.

## Apple Watch

When you dictate on Apple Watch, the recording is sent directly to VoxBoard on
your own paired iPhone over Apple's secure WatchConnectivity link, transcribed
there, and the text is sent back to your Watch. It does not pass through any
VoxBoard server.

## Keyboard "Full Access"

iOS asks you to allow Full Access for VoxBoard's keyboard. VoxBoard uses it only
to share settings and the transcribed text between the keyboard and the
VoxBoard app on the same device, and to give haptic feedback. The keyboard does
not send anything you type over the network.

## Microphone

VoxBoard uses the microphone only to record what you dictate. When instant
keyboard dictation is on, the VoxBoard app keeps its microphone session ready
after a keyboard recording, for a time you choose, so the next recording can
start without switching apps. Nothing is recorded until you tap the microphone
button. iOS shows its orange indicator while the session is ready, and you can
turn it off from the keyboard or in Settings at any time.

## Network access

VoxBoard connects to the internet only to download the speech and text models
you choose, from https://huggingface.co/devoar/voxboard-models. These downloads
contain no personal information.

## Children

VoxBoard does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children.
